Study Notes on Galatians

Paul’s Missionary Journeys: A Detailed Timeline

Early life

Paul: born in Tarsus

References: Acts 21:39; Acts 22:3

Early life

Paul: brought up in Jerusalem

References: Acts 22:3

c. AD 32/33 medium confidence

Damascus: conversion and call

Place: Damascus

References: Acts 9:1–22; Galatians 1:15–16; 1 Corinthians 9:1; 15:8-9

Soon after conversion; duration unknown high sequence / low date

Arabia

Place: Arabia, then Damascus

References: Galatians 1:16–17

Before the first Jerusalem visit high sequence

Damascus: early preaching

Place: Damascus

References: Galatians 1:17; Acts 9:23–25; 2 Corinthians 11:32-33

c. AD 35/36; three years after conversion medium confidence

Jerusalem: first visit after conversion

Place: Jerusalem

References: Galatians 1:18–20; Acts 9:26–30

c. AD 36–46 medium confidence

Syria and Cilicia

Region / place: Syria, Cilicia, Tarsus

References: Galatians 1:21–24; Acts 9:30; 11:25

c. AD 44–46 medium confidence

Antioch of Syria: teaching and commissioning

References: Acts 11:26; 13:1–3

c. AD 46–47 medium confidence

First journey: Cyprus

Route: Antioch → Seleucia → Salamis → Paphos

References: Acts 13:1–12

c. AD 46–47 medium confidence

First journey: southern Galatia

Route: Perga → Pisidian Antioch → Iconium → Lystra → Derbe

References: Acts 13:13–14:20; Galatians 4:13–14

c. AD 47–48 high sequence

First journey: return and Antioch

Route: Derbe → Lystra → Iconium → Pisidian Antioch → Perga → Attalia → Antioch

References: Acts 14:21–28

c. AD 48/49 medium confidence

Jerusalem: the apostolic council

Place: Jerusalem

References: Acts 15:1–35; Galatians 2:1–10

c. AD 49–50 high sequence

Second journey: Syria, Cilicia, and Galatia

Route: Antioch → Syria and Cilicia → Derbe → Lystra → Phrygia and Galatia

References: Acts 15:36–16:6

c. AD 50 medium confidence

Second journey: Troas and Macedonia

Route: Troas → Samothrace → Neapolis → Philippi → Amphipolis → Apollonia → Thessalonica → Berea

References: Acts 16:7–17:14

c. AD 50–52; eighteen months in Corinth high confidence

Second journey: Athens and Corinth

Route: Athens → Corinth

References: Acts 17:15–18:17; 18:11; 1 Thessalonians 1:1; 2 Thessalonians 1:1

c. AD 52 medium confidence

Second journey: return through Ephesus and Antioch

Route: Corinth → Cenchreae → Ephesus → Caesarea → Jerusalem → Antioch

References: Acts 18:18–22

c. AD 53 medium confidence

Third journey: Galatia and Phrygia

Route: Antioch → Galatia and Phrygia

References: Acts 18:23

c. AD 53–55; about three years high duration

Third journey: Ephesus

Place: Ephesus

References: Acts 19:1–20:1, 31; 1 Corinthians 16:8–9

c. AD 55–57 medium confidence

Third journey: Macedonia, Greece, and the Aegean coast

Route: Ephesus → Macedonia → Greece → Philippi → Troas → Assos → Mitylene → Chios → Samos → Miletus

References: Acts 20:1–16; Romans 15:25–28; 1 Corinthians 16:5–8

c. AD 57 medium confidence

Jerusalem: arrest at the end of the third journey

Route: Miletus → Cos → Rhodes → Patara → Tyre → Ptolemais → Caesarea → Jerusalem

References: Acts 20:17–21:36

c. AD 57–59; two years high duration

Caesarea: imprisonment and hearings

Place: Caesarea Maritima

References: Acts 23:23–26:32; 24:27; 25:10–12

c. AD 59–60; two years in Rome medium confidence

Rome: voyage and house arrest

Route: Caesarea → Sidon → Myra → Fair Havens → Malta → Syracuse → Rhegium → Puteoli → Rome

References: Acts 27:1–28:31
