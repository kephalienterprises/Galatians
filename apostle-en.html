Study Notes on Galatians

What is an Apostle?

From the very beginning of his letter to the Galatians, Paul defends his apostleship. In Galatians 1:1, he identifies himself as an apostle whose commission comes neither from men nor through a man, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father. So, what is an apostle?
